How it works

Easy4–5 letters
Medium6–8 letters
Hard9–12 letters

Scoring: On a win, (10 × distinct correct letters + 10 × attempts left) × level multiplier. Multipliers: Easy ×1, Medium ×1.5, Hard ×2. A loss scores 0. Duplicate guesses change nothing.

Words are selected from a server-side SQLite dictionary. Scores are saved to the shared leaderboard.
